Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following mobile games published on Google Play by Damian Rokita ("the developer", "we"):

  • Snow War
  • Everward

We do not require you to create an account in any of our games. Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. We do not ask for your name, contacts or other personal details. The only exception is the optional email address you may choose to include when you send a bug report from Everward, as described below.

Snow War

Snow War does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal information. It does not display advertisements and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. All gameplay takes place locally on your device.

Everward

Everward is free to play and is supported by advertising. An optional subscription purchased through Google Play may be offered to remove ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Everward uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

Data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings.

Crash reporting, diagnostics and bug reports (Sentry)

Everward uses Sentry, an error-monitoring service provided by Functional Software, Inc. ("Sentry"), to detect and fix crashes and other technical problems. Sentry acts as a service provider that processes this data on our behalf and only according to our instructions; we do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

When the game crashes or encounters an error, the following data may be sent automatically to Sentry:

  • Crash logs and error details, such as stack traces and the sequence of in-game events leading up to the error
  • Diagnostic information, such as game version, device model, Android version, memory and performance data
  • A random installation identifier generated by the game, used to tell whether several errors come from the same installation. It is not your advertising ID, is not linked to your identity, and is reset when you uninstall the game or clear its data

If you choose to send a bug report from within the game, it will also include the description you write and, only if you decide to provide it, your email address. Providing an email address is optional; we use it only to reply to you about your report.

This data is used solely to keep the game working properly, analyse and fix errors, and respond to bug reports. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Crash and diagnostic data is kept only as long as needed for these purposes and is deleted automatically within 90 days. Sentry's handling of data is described in the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Requesting deletion of your data

You can request deletion of the data Everward has sent to us at any time, without uninstalling the game:

  1. Send an email to damianrokita92@gmail.com with the subject "Everward data deletion".
  2. If you sent a bug report, include the email address you provided (if any) and the approximate date of the report, so we can find it.
  3. We will delete the matching data and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted: bug reports (your description), the email address you provided, and crash logs and diagnostic data linked to them. We do not keep any of this data after deletion. Crash and diagnostic data that cannot be linked to a request is deleted automatically within 90 days.

Data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings and manage your data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Subscription

If offered, the ad-free subscription is purchased and managed through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we do not receive or store your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Children's privacy

Everward is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

External links

A game may contain links that open external websites in your browser. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to request deletion of your data, contact us at damianrokita92@gmail.com.
