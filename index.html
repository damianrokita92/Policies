Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following mobile games published on Google Play by Damian Rokita ("the developer", "we"):

  • Snow War

We do not require you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts or other personal details in any of our games. Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Snow War

Snow War does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal information. It does not display advertisements and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. All gameplay takes place locally on your device.

Everward

Everward does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal information. The current version does not display advertisements and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. All gameplay takes place locally on your device.

If a future version of Everward adds advertising or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

Everward is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
