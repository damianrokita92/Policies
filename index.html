Information we collect

The games do not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal information of their users. We do not require you to create an account or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (for example saved games, sound and language preferences) are stored only locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The games do not display advertisements and do not use any analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Permissions

The games do not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.

External links

A game may contain links that open external websites in your browser. Those websites are not operated by us and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Since the games do not collect any personal information, they do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
